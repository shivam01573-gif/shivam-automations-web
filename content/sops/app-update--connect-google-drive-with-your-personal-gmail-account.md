# Connect Google Drive with Personal Gmail Account using Make.com: A Step-by-Step Guide
## Overview
The integration of Google Drive with personal Gmail accounts is a crucial aspect of business automation, enabling seamless file sharing and collaboration. Make.com, a leading automation platform, has recently announced an update to support this integration. This guide will walk you through the process of connecting Google Drive with your personal Gmail account using Make.com, resolving the issue of limited access to Google Drive connections.

## Error Diagnostic
The core issue here is not an error, but rather an update to support personal Gmail accounts for Google Drive connections. The software systems involved include:
* Make.com: An automation platform for integrating various applications and services.
* Google Drive: A cloud storage service for storing and sharing files.
* Gmail: A personal email service provided by Google.
The root cause is the lack of technical issue or API sync failure, but rather an update to support personal Gmail accounts for Google Drive connections.

## Step-by-Step Resolution
### Step 1: Create a Make.com Account
Sign up for a Make.com account if you haven't already, and log in to your dashboard.
### Step 2: Connect Google Drive to Make.com
Click on the "Modules" tab and search for Google Drive. Select the "Google Drive" module and click on the "Connect" button. Follow the prompts to authenticate your Google Drive account.
### Step 3: Connect Gmail to Make.com
Click on the "Modules" tab and search for Gmail. Select the "Gmail" module and click on the "Connect" button. Follow the prompts to authenticate your Gmail account.
### Step 4: Configure the Integration
Create a new scenario in Make.com and add the Google Drive and Gmail modules. Configure the modules to integrate your Google Drive and Gmail accounts, enabling seamless file sharing and collaboration.
### Step 5: Verify the Integration
Test the integration by sharing a file from Google Drive to Gmail using the Make.com scenario. Verify that the file is successfully shared and received in your Gmail account.
