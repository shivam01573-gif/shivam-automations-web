## Overview
The increasing complexity of B2B integrations often involves multiple software systems, including Make.com, a popular automation platform. A common pain point faced by users is being locked out of their old account due to email account changes, leading to authentication failures. This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) aims to provide a comprehensive guide to resolving this issue, ensuring seamless integration and automation of business processes.

## Error Diagnostic
The error diagnostic reveals that the core issue is a "Failed to log in due to email account change," with the root cause guessed as "Authentication failure due to missing or outdated authentication credentials." This indicates that the authentication credentials used for the integration are no longer valid, likely due to the email account change. The software list includes Make.com, suggesting that the integration is built using this platform.

## Step-by-Step Resolution
### Step 1: Update Authentication Credentials
Update the authentication credentials in Make.com to reflect the new email account. This involves accessing the Make.com dashboard, navigating to the integration settings, and updating the email address associated with the account.

### Step 2: Verify Email Address
Verify the new email address by clicking on the verification link sent by Make.com. This step is crucial to ensure that the new email address is validated and can be used for authentication.

### Step 3: Re-establish Integration
Re-establish the integration by re-configuring the connection between Make.com and the other software systems involved. This may involve re-entering the API keys, access tokens, or other authentication credentials.

### Step 4: Test the Integration
Test the integration to ensure that it is working as expected. This involves running a test scenario, verifying that data is being exchanged correctly, and checking for any error messages.
